Privacy Policy — Trace & Sound Farm

Effective date: 12 November 2026 · App: Trace & Sound Farm (com.tracepace.tracefarm) · Developer: TracePace · Contact: hicksjaredc@gmail.com

The short version. Trace & Sound Farm is a paid, 100% offline app for young children. It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. It has no internet permission at all. There are no ads, no analytics, and no third-party tracking SDKs.

1. Information we collect

We collect no personal information. The app does not ask for, and cannot access, names, email addresses, birth dates, photos, contacts, location, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or any other personal data.

2. Network access

The app's Android manifest declares no INTERNET permission. The application is technically incapable of making network requests. There is no account system, no cloud sync, and no remote configuration.

3. Data stored on your device

The only data the app stores is a small list of the sticker-book items your child has earned. This list is held in the app's private storage on the device and is never uploaded. Uninstalling the app removes it. You can also clear it in-app from the Sticker Book screen (“Clear stickers”).

4. Advertising and third-party SDKs

The app contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, no social or login SDKs, and no in-app purchases. The only native code is a small bridge that plays bundled audio files and reads/writes the on-device sticker list.

5. Children's privacy (COPPA)

Trace & Sound Farm is designed for children ages 3 and up. Because the app collects no personal information and has no network capability, it does not collect personal information from children under the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) or comparable laws. Parents and guardians may contact us at the address above with any questions.

6. Your choices

Because no personal data is collected, there is nothing to access, correct, export, or delete on our servers. To remove on-device data, clear the sticker book in-app or uninstall the app.

7. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes any of the above, this policy will be updated and the effective date revised before release.
